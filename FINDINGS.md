# Findings journal

Append-only. Newest at the bottom. This is where every observation about the
market goes, and the only place a research claim may live.

**There is no standard of proof in this repository yet.** That is deliberate —
see `RIZ.md`. Nothing here is a proven effect and nothing here licenses a trade.
Write what you looked at, what you saw, and on which data. Do not dress an
observation up as a result.

## How to write an entry

```
### <date> · <short title>
**Looked at:** instrument, timeframes, slice of history, how many zones.
**Question:** what was being asked.
**Saw:** what came out, in numbers.
**Definitions used:** any derived term and its exact meaning here.
**Doubts:** what could explain this other than the market.
```

Keep it short. If an entry needs a page, the question was too big.

## Open questions

Operator observations and hunches, not yet measured. Each is a candidate for an
entry above.

- **Stacked timeframes pull hard.** When roughly three zones from different
  timeframes sit in about the same price pocket, price returns there almost
  every time, and fast. Needs "roughly the same pocket" defined before it can be
  measured at all.
- **Width governs the aftermath.** Wider zones behave differently from narrow
  ones — plausibly less likely to be revisited. Condition on `zone_width`
  before asking any other question about a zone.
- **Where the setup lives.** Three distinct places to look: before price reaches
  the line, at the moment of contact, and after T0. These are different setups
  and should not be pooled.
- **Flicker.** Zones whose T0 never reached native confirmation exist in the
  field but never appeared on a closed chart. Do they behave differently?
